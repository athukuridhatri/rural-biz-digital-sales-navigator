# 🌾 Rural Biz Digital Sales Navigator

An AI-powered solution designed to help rural women entrepreneurs navigate digital sales, understand customer preferences, discover product opportunities, and make better business decisions.

## 🚀 Live Demo

👉 [Try the Rural Biz Digital Sales Navigator](https://partyrock.aws/u/Dhatri123/pU9XlAvzH/RuralBiz-Digital-Sales-Navigator)

## 💡 About the Project

Rural women entrepreneurs often face challenges in understanding digital markets, customer preferences, product demand, and online sales opportunities.

The **Rural Biz Digital Sales Navigator** uses Generative AI to provide simple and practical business guidance, helping entrepreneurs make informed decisions and explore opportunities for digital growth.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🤖 AI-powered business guidance
- 📊 Customer preference insights
- 🛍️ Product recommendations
- 📱 Digital sales guidance
- 💡 Business-growth suggestions
- 🌱 Support for rural women entrepreneurs

## 🛠️ Built With

- Amazon PartyRock
- Generative AI
- Amazon Bedrock

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many rural women entrepreneurs have limited access to digital tools and personalized guidance for understanding customers, improving sales, and expanding their businesses.

## 💡 Solution

The Rural Biz Digital Sales Navigator provides AI-powered recommendations in an easy-to-use format.

It helps entrepreneurs:

1. Understand customer preferences
2. Identify suitable products
3. Explore digital sales opportunities
4. Improve their business strategies
5. Make better data-informed decisions
